# Spring Walleye Watch: source and interpretation contract

Research checkpoint: November 17, 2026.
The existing Saginaw Bay walleye page remains the canonical source for its current fishing-hub information. The spring river watch is a separate, limited set of environmental observations and historical fisheries references, not an actual-fish-detection product.

## Live instrument inventory

- Saginaw River at Saginaw, Michigan: USGS 04157005
- Tittabawassee River at Midland, Michigan: USGS 04156000
- Detroit River at Fort Wayne, Michigan: USGS 04165710
- Maumee River at Waterville, Ohio: USGS 04193500
- Sandusky River near Fremont, Ohio: USGS 04198000

Observed flow, temperature and their timestamps are fetched by the existing USGS sensor adapter. Temperature must be observed within six hours to be reported; otherwise the output is null. A gauge is a local instrument, not a direct statement about any fishing location up- or downstream. Some stations may not offer temperature.

## Fisheries evidence

- Michigan DNR weekly fishing reports: https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/things-to-do/fishing/weekly . The DNR posted that the report would be suspended after November 4, 2026 pending more contributions. Do not promise weekly updates during this gap.
- Detroit River 2026 season review: https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/about/newsroom/releases/2026/06/05/dnr-reminds-anglers-about-walleye-possession-limit-on-detroit-river . The DNR confirms fishing activity during spring 2026, not spring 2027.
- Maumee River spring background, ODNR 2022 guide: https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/wildlife/fish-management/036IL%20-%20Maumee%20River%20Walleye%20-%202022_FINAL.pdf . ODNR guidance covers typical spawning and access context; it does not measure current fish presence.
- Maumee and Sandusky general river-run season description, ODNR Lake Erie Fishing Guide: https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/wildlife/fishing/Pub5276_LakeErieFishingGuide_Web.pdf . Undated guidance, not a catch observation.
- Ohio fishing-report portal indexed a Maumee/Sandusky report updated April 14, 2026, but direct retrieval was not reliable at this checkpoint. We do not promote it as a current accessible source link.

No 2027 fish-presence statement may be generated from these historical records. The seasonal temperature bands in the current software are descriptive screening labels, not calibrated walleye-run probabilities. A fishing report from a current year is still limited to its original river, area and observation period. Current regulations must be consulted by state and section, not copied from an old guide.

## Evidence promotion gate

Before any claim of recent fishery activity: capture an actual dated authority report that specifically names walleye and the river; record its original URL and publication time; independently verify the passage; record who reviewed it; ensure reported sightings/catches are not conflated with spawning; expire its active status within 14 days and never continue a current-season claim after rollover. Human review and CI are required before promotion.

No AI-generated catch reports, hallucinated water temperatures, guaranteed runs, or stale report relabeling.
